# Project 3: Responsible AI Policy & Operating Model

## Scenario

**Organisation:** Acme Financial Services  
**System under review:** Human-centred AI services used in lending, insurance, and customer support  
**Objective:** Define the governance rules that turn AI principles into accountable day-to-day controls

Acme has completed its AI inventory and risk classification work. The next step is organisational governance: determining who is accountable for AI risk, which principles apply across the lifecycle, and how controls are operationalised across product, legal, data, and business teams.

---

## What This Project Demonstrates

- Ability to translate AI principles into a concrete operating model
- Understanding of the NIST AI RMF **Govern** function
- Practical governance design for accountability, escalation, and review
- Awareness that responsible AI is not a policy document alone; it is a control system

## Frameworks Applied

- **NIST AI RMF** — Govern
- **EU AI Act** — Articles 9, 14, 26, 27, 29, and 52
- **ISO/IEC 42001** — AI management system concepts

---

## Why This Matter

The strongest governance programmes do not stop at risk classification. They create a durable organisational rulebook: who decides, what is allowed, what evidence is required, and how incidents are escalated. This project demonstrates that design in a practical way.
