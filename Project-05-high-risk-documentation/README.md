# Project 5: High-Risk AI Documentation & Conformity Pack

## Scenario

**Organisation:** Acme Financial Services  
**System:** TalentMatch AI — AI-assisted recruitment and candidate shortlisting  
**Status:** High-risk AI system under the EU AI Act requiring documentation and governance evidence

This project creates the documentation pack expected for a high-risk AI system. It does not replace formal legal review or certification, but it demonstrates the core record set organisations need to show they have designed and operated the system responsibly.

---

## What This Project Demonstrates

- Ability to produce EU AI Act-aligned documentation for a high-risk system
- Understanding of technical documentation, data governance, and human oversight requirements
- Practical translation of governance obligations into evidence artefacts
- Capacity to prepare evidence that can support management review and regulator inquiries

## Frameworks Applied

- **EU AI Act** — Articles 9-17, and Annexes
- **NIST AI RMF** — Measure and Manage
- **ISO 31000** — Risk and control documentation

---

## Why This Matter

High-risk AI systems are not judged merely by technical sophistication. They are judged by the quality of the documentation, controls, governance evidence, and accountability structure behind them. This project shows what that evidence pack looks like in practice.
