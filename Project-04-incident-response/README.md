# Project 4: AI Incident Response & Regulatory Escalation

## Scenario

**Organisation:** Acme Financial Services  
**Issue:** AI customer service assistant and lending decision support model experienced a material failure in production

A customer-facing AI assistant began offering inconsistent financial advice, and a lending support model unexpectedly increased approval rates for a sensitive customer segment. The issue was not immediately reported as a formal AI incident and required rapid escalation to legal, compliance, and risk teams.

---

## What This Project Demonstrates

- Ability to identify an AI incident and apply a structured response process
- Understanding of regulatory escalation needs under AI governance and consumer protection law
- Practical use of root-cause analysis thinking in a governance context
- Capacity to communicate decisions and timelines to executives and regulators

## Frameworks Applied

- **NIST AI RMF** — Manage
- **EU AI Act** — Articles 9, 14, 15, and 26
- **ISO 31000** — Risk event handling and escalation principles

---

## Why This Matter

Most AI governance failures are not caused by a single technical bug alone. They are often caused by weak monitoring, unclear ownership, and delayed escalation. This project demonstrates how a well-designed incident-response framework reduces harm and improves credibility with regulators.
